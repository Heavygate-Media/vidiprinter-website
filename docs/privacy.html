Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Updated 18 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Your settings stay on your iPhone, apart from what alerts need.
  • Apple takes your payment. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.
  • Unless you turn it off, the app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can improve it.

1. Who we are

Vidiprinter+ is made by Michael McCarthy, trading as Heavygate Media, in the United Kingdom ("we"). We're the controller of the personal data described here under UK data protection law. Contact us at support@vidiprinterplus.app.

2. What we collect

Your subscription. When you subscribe or open the app, it sends our server the signed purchase record Apple gives it. We keep a transaction identifier, the plan, its status and expiry date, and Apple tells us when it renews, lapses or is refunded.

Using the app. The app's requests carry a session key so our server can check you're subscribed. The server counts requests for a few minutes to prevent abuse, and our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs, which can include internet addresses.

Alerts and Live Activities, only if you turn them on: the push addresses Apple gives the app, the clubs, competitions and events you want alerts for, clubs you've hidden, and the matches you follow live.

Usage statistics, unless you turn them off in Settings → Share anonymous usage: which screens and features are used, how far people get through setup, and once a day a summary of how the app is set up (for example, how many clubs you follow, which competitions, which alerts, and whether you use widgets and Live Activities). Each also carries the app and iOS version, the iPhone model, the language and the country. They carry no name, account or device identifier, the internet address isn't stored, and they can't be linked to you or to each other beyond a single day.

Everything else (the look, text size, your clubs and competitions, how names are shown) stays on your iPhone. We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or browsing history, and we never sell personal data.

3. Players and coaches

The app shows publicly reported information about professional footballers and coaches: names, nationality, clubs, match events and statistics, and records built from past results. It comes from a licensed football-data provider. We don't hold contact details or health information. If you're a player or coach, you can object or ask for a correction at support@vidiprinterplus.app.

5. Who handles it

  • Apple takes payments, sends our server your purchase records, and delivers notifications.
  • Our hosting provider runs our server and database in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Our analytics provider stores the usage statistics in the EU.

UK law treats the EU as protecting data adequately. Our hosting provider is based in the USA, and its staff may access the server from there, under the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. A suspended copy of our database from its earlier US hosting will be deleted on 30 November 2026.

6. How long we keep it

  • Subscription records: while you're subscribed, then so a renewal or refund can be matched. Ask and we'll delete it.
  • Alert and Live Activity data: until you turn both off, Apple says the app's gone, or the app hasn't been opened for 60 days.
  • Usage statistics: up to five years.
  • Request counts: a few minutes. Server logs: a short period.
  • Support emails: up to two years after we've helped you.
  • Financial records: as long as UK tax law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or limit the use of your data, object to its use, or have it sent to you or another provider. Email support@vidiprinterplus.app: we'll reply within a month, free of charge. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Children

Vidiprinter+ isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app before any significant change to how we use your data.
